FU · Interactive sales protocol

Appointment setter — step by step · exportable handoff for the salesperson.

0 · Client & meeting

Protocol: Document who you are speaking with before you go into Why.

1 · Start with WHY

Ask: “What is the main reason you are considering [country]?” — let the client talk. Choose country in step 0.

2 · Location

Ask: “Which areas are you mainly considering?” + “What makes those interesting?”

3 · Budget without locking early

Ask: Fixed budget, or flexible depending on property type and potential? Separate budget-driven vs value-driven.

4 · Property type

Ask: Ready to use now, or a new build where preferences can be shaped?

5 · Own use & rental

Ask: Combine own use with rental when they are not there?

6 · Motivation & decision

Ask: “What would make you say in three years: this was a really good decision?”

7 · Handoff to salesperson

Outcome: Three needs + emotional driver + focus. The salesperson does not start from zero.

Do not sell the property — sell the solution to the need. Leave blanks empty. No promised returns.
